Privacy Policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

How Algazy Group Inc. handles the information you share with us when you ask about a kitchen remodel or hire us to build one.

On this page

The Short Version What We Collect How We Use It Who We Share It With Cookies How Long We Keep It Your Choices and California Rights Security Children Changes to This Policy Contact Us

The Short Version

  • We collect only what you give us to reply to a quote request, plus the basic technical data any website receives.
  • We use it to talk with you about your kitchen and, if you hire us, to do the work.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising.
  • This site uses no advertising or tracking cookies.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete what we hold about you.

What We Collect

What you send us. When you request a quote, call, text or email us, we receive what you choose to share: usually your name, phone number, email address, the area you live in and details about your kitchen project, such as the layout, the work you want and when you hope to start.

During a project. If you hire us, we also keep your address, the contract, drawings, photos of the site, permit records and payment records.

Technical data. Like any website, the server that hosts this site receives your IP address, browser type and the pages you request. The fonts on this site load from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address when a page opens.

How We Use It

  • To reply to your request, arrange a visit and prepare an estimate.
  • To plan, permit and build your project, and to keep you informed while we do.
  • To keep the records that contractors in California must keep, such as contracts, permits and warranty information.
  • To keep this website working and secure.

We do not send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who We Share It With

We share only what is needed to do the job:

  • Our trades and suppliers, such as cabinet makers, electricians and plumbers working on your project.
  • Building departments, such as LADBS or your city, which need project details to issue permits and inspect the work.
  • Service providers that host this site or handle our phone, text and email.
  • Authorities or advisers when the law requires it, or to protect our rights.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Your browser may keep ordinary copies of the pages and images to load them faster next time.

How Long We Keep It

If you do not hire us, we delete quote requests within two years. If you do, we keep project records for as long as warranty, tax and licensing rules require, which in California can be ten years or more after the work is finished.

Your Choices and California Rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information, unless we must keep it by law or to finish work you asked for;
  • stop calling or texting you. Reply STOP to any text, or just tell us.

California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act where it applies, and we will not treat you differently for using them. To make a request, call or text (323) 940-8041. We may need to confirm your identity first.

Security

We limit who can see your information to the people who need it for your project, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of sending or storing data is perfectly secure, so please do not send payment card numbers by text or email.

Children

This site is for homeowners and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top.

Contact Us

Questions about your privacy? Call or text Algazy Group Inc. at (323) 940-8041.
